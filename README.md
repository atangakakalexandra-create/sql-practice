* Malaria Cases Analysis (SQL)
Analysis of malaria incidence (new cases per 1,000 people at risk) across countries, 2000-2024, using SQL.

* Data source
Our World in Data, from WHO via the World Bank (CC BY): https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/incidence-of-malaria

* Tools
SQL (SQLite)

* Skills shown
SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY, LIMIT, COUNT, DISTINCT, GROUP BY, HAVING, AVG, subqueries

* Findings
1. Nigeria's malaria cases went from 401.79 per 1,000 in 2000 to 294.25 per 1,000 in 2024.
2. The three countries with the highest average malaria cases were Burkina Faso (475.03), Central African Republic (419.87) and Benin (419.37).
3. Nigeria's best year was 2015 (283.81, fewest cases) and its worst year was 2008 (402.01, most cases).
